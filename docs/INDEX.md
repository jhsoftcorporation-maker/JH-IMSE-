## 🏆 ইতিমধ্যে সমাপ্ত ও লাইভ ফেজসমূহ (Phases Completed: 1 to 24)

1. **Phase 1-9:** Core Multi-Tenant Engine, POS, CRM, Super Admin, HRM, Storefront & MySQL.
2. **Phase 10:** Automated Double-Entry Accounting Journal, Chart of Accounts, Trial Balance, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow & Bank Reconciliation.
3. **Phase 11:** Central Warehouse & Multi-Branch Logistics (Branch Requisitions, Transfer Challans, GIT, GRN & Branch P&L).
4. **Phase 12:** AI Demand Forecasting, Run-Out Dates, Dynamic ROP, Auto-PO Generation & Dead Stock Liquidation.
5. **Phase 13:** Global Commerce Engine (USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR, BDT), Live Forex, Stripe/PayPal, Cross-Border Taxes & NBR Export Invoices.
6. **Phase 14:** Omnichannel WhatsApp Cloud API Hub, Meta Verified Templates, 2-Way Interactive Bot Simulator & VIP Loyalty Rewards Tiers.
7. **Phase 15:** Sunmi & iMin POS Native Hardware Integration (Direct ESC/POS Thermal Print, Electronic Cash Drawer Kick via RJ11 pulse, Dual-Screen CFD with bKash Dynamic QR, RS-232/USB Weighing Scale).
8. **Phase 16:** Mobile-First Adaptive Shell, Theme Studio & Dynamic Appearance (5-tab bottom navigation bar, gradient welcome card, 3-column launcher grid, nested accordion drawer, dynamic appearance palette `#663399`, and mobile POS shift register).
9. **Phase 17:** Brands, Sub-Categories, Units of Measure (UOM), Opening Stock Setup & Barcode Label Sheets Generator.
10. **Phase 18:** Returns Management (RMA / Credit Notes), Purchase Returns, Damage Write-Offs & Physical Stock Count / Audits.
11. **Phase 19:** Multi-Tier Sales Agent Commission Engine, Commission Programs, Calculation Rules, Voucher Receipts & Pay-Outs.
12. **Phase 20:** Service & Repair Jobs, Device Intake Diagnostics, QA Inspection Checklists, AMC Contracts & IMEI / Serial Warranty Tracker.
13. **Phase 21:** Fixed Asset Register, Depreciation Engine (Straight-Line SLM & Written-Down Value WDV) & Asset Maintenance / Repair Logs.
14. **Phase 22:** Project Management, Task Kanban Board, Commercial Contracts & Appointment Bookings Hub.
15. **Phase 23:** People Management, Customer Segmentation (With vs Without Login), B2B Invitation Codes Engine, Pending Applications Queue & Client Self-Service Portal Simulator.
16. **Phase 24:** 3D Interactive Spatial Sales & Multi-Branch Dashboard, Cash Register Shift Reports & Z-Report Reconciliation with Physical Denomination Counting, Inventory Intelligence Radar (Negative Stock, Stock Aging 0-180+ Days, Expiry Register), Customer/Supplier Ranking Matrix, and User Device Login Audit & System Error Exception Hub.
